ZFO2GL performance: GL report from 10–15 minutes to seconds

“Above & Beyond” award · ABAP Performance

Challenge

ZFO2GL, a complex general ledger dump report, took 10–15 minutes per run, and finance users depended on it repeatedly.

Solution

Redesigned the data processing at AMDP level, converting a table-function AMDP into a standard AMDP so the heavy logic runs inside SAP HANA.

Result

Runtime cut from 10–15 minutes to seconds on every run, a time saving that adds up for the business with each use. Recognised with an “Above & Beyond” award.

  • AMDP
  • SQLScript
  • SAP HANA
  • ABAP

Separating EWM and TM after a system upgrade

EWM · TM

Challenge

After the 2025 upgrade, deliveries for a food manufacturing business started defaulting to TM-managed, while the business needs EWM and TM to run separately. Once such a delivery reaches EWM it can’t be changed, so warehouse and transport operations were at risk of stopping.

Solution

Diagnosed the root cause and built a permanent enhancement within 2 hours. It intercepts the delivery before it reaches EWM and removes the unwanted EWM–TM link. Control values live in TVARVC, so no logic is hardcoded.

Result

Downtime prevented across all affected plants, with EWM and TM running independently as the business intended.

  • Enhancement framework
  • Outbound delivery
  • EWM
  • TM
  • TVARVC

PO approval with mobile push notifications and AI agents

SAP BTP · Fiori · AI

Challenge

Approval requests arrived by email, where they were easily missed or buried, which held up purchase orders. Approvers also had to open and investigate each PO before deciding.

Solution

When a PO is created in S/4HANA, the event flows through SAP Event Mesh to a CAP app. An AI agent analyses the PO, the approval matrix picks the approver, and a mobile push notification through Joule lands on the approver’s phone with the analysis and approve or hold options. A Fiori inbox works on both desktop and mobile. A second agent, built on CAP with MCP and Gemini, answers PO questions in a chat and fetches PO details on request.

Result

Like a WhatsApp message, the notification gets seen right away instead of sitting unread in email, and the approver decides from it with the analysis already done. The AI analysis is a working proof of concept on top of the approval flow.

  • SAP CAP
  • SAPUI5 / Fiori
  • SAP Event Mesh
  • Joule mobile notifications
  • MCP
  • Gemini
  • React

Stopping over-quantity deliveries at the source

EWM · MM

Challenge

Outbound deliveries exceeding stock transport order quantities got stuck in EWM queues. Clearing each case took 1–2 days of coordination between MM and EWM teams.

Solution

Implemented a BAdI that blocks goods issue when the delivery quantity exceeds the PO quantity, so the problem is caught immediately instead of surfacing later in the queue.

Result

Queue blockages from over-quantity deliveries eliminated, along with the recurring manual monitoring.

  • BAdI
  • Goods issue
  • EWM queues
  • Stock transport orders

EWM RF: carton-by-carton scanning and custom function keys

EWM RF

Challenge

Operators at a food manufacturing business needed to confirm quantities by scanning each carton’s ID (CID): as many cartons as the quantity, that many scans.

Solution

Custom RFUI screens and logic that wrap the standard RF function modules, enforce the scan count, and handle fractional and damaged cartons.
